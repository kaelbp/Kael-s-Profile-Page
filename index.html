Passion Projects




WORLDBREAKER

Roles: Author, Illustrator & Colorist | 2026

On June 10, 2026, I was invited to join Worldbreaker, an independent creative team focusing on developing and sharing original stories through writing and visual storytelling, particularly comics.


During my time with the team, I primarily worked as the coloring artist for one of its upcoming original webcomic series. I also contributed some official illustrations and assets, and worked closely with the project manager, as well the team's 3D background artist.


Balancing this passion work with my studies was challenging, but it also gave me the opportunity to further develop my skills. It was a meaningful experience that allowed me to share what I knew while also learning from the people I worked with.




Animatics

紗痲 (shama) | Valen • Junko | KCHAELT | 2025

An original character (OC) animatic I made as a personal project, mostly for my own enjoyment. It features my original characters, Valen and Junko, in a recreation of the animated music video (AMV) Shama by Nilfruits and WOOMA. It was one of the short projects I committed to fully out of my passion for my characters.


All the illustrations and assets in the video, except for the subtitles or typography, were manually made and hand drawn. The whole video and sequence were also manually edited by yours truly.




Digital Illustrations

KCHAELT (Previously Kioshirei) | 2016 - Present

I've always loved drawing, especially having grown up as a fan of anime and manga. As a child, I've always dreamed to become a really good illustrator to pursue a career in those industries.


Though, over time, I began to see being an illustrator as more than just something I wanted to be. It eventually became a way for me to tell stories and express emotions through drawings, even without words. And I've come to love that.


If there's something I would love to do for the rest of my life, it's gotta be expressing emotions and stories through illustrations.
